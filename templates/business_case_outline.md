# Business case outline

The structure used for `excel/business_case.xlsx` (P2: North water heater supply). Use the same structure for any operating decision.

1. **Question.** One sentence: what is being decided.
2. **Cost of doing nothing.** Sized from last year's data, with the view it comes from.
3. **Options.** Two to four, each described in one line in the business's own terms.
4. **Assumptions.** Every number that is not from the data (capex, life of an asset, carrying rate, warehouse cost) on one sheet, in yellow cells, with who provided it.
5. **Annual cost of each option.** Built from live formulas only, so changing an assumption updates the answer.
6. **Ranking and recommendation.** The cheapest option, chosen by formula, and the payback against the next best.
7. **Sensitivity.** The one or two assumptions the answer depends on most, tested over a realistic range. State where the recommendation would change.
8. **Risks and what is not included.** What the model leaves out and why.
9. **Owner and next step.** Who acts, and by when.
