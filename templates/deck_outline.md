# Leadership deck outline

The structure used for `deck/orvane_fy26_leadership_review.pptx`. Six to eight slides, insight first, method in the speaker notes.

| Slide | Content | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title: period, scope, purpose of the meeting | State the decision the meeting is for |
| 2 | Summary: the three or four issues in ₹, and the decisions asked for | Readable on its own if nothing else is read |
| 3–5 | One finding per slide | Title is the message as a sentence; one chart; three or four supporting points |
| 6 | Recommendation or business case | Options compared on one measure; payback; what the answer depends on |
| 7 | Early signals | Things to watch that are not yet in the numbers |
| 8 | Next steps | Action, owner, value |

- Every number on a slide comes from a view or a file in the repo; the speaker notes say which.
- Caveats go in the speaker notes, not in small print on the slide.
- One colour for "bad" and one for "good" across the whole deck.
